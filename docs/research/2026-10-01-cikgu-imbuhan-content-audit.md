- `imbuhan-men` **memfoto**: Kamus Dewan Edisi Keempat "mengambil gambar dgn kamera" (PRPM snippet `Cari1?keyword=memfoto&d=73980`). Real word. *memveto* was cleared in file 1.

- `imbuhan-i` **memukuli** ("memukul berkali-kali; menghajar"), **menciumi** ("mencium berulang kali; ki menjejaki") — Kamus-Dewan-form definitions found via search snippets (not a PRPM page; a KBBI-only word would not carry the *ki* label).
- `imbuhan-an` **kuburan** "cemetery/burial place": attested "tanah tempat menguburkan mayat"; *perkuburan* is the commoner Malaysian form but kuburan is a Kamus Dewan headword.
- `imbuhan-pen` **pelawan, perawat, pemukul, pengambil, pengira, pengecat**: all regular peN- agent/instrument nouns with Kamus Dewan entries (pemukul "alat utk memukul; orang yg memukul", pengecat "orang yg mengecat; alat …").
- `imbuhan-ber` **beranak** (mempunyai anak), **berbaju, bertopi, bercahaya, berkenalan**; `imbuhan-ke-an` **ketuhanan, kebudayaan, kematian, kedatangan, kehilangan, kemudahan**; `imbuhan-kan` **mengambilkan** ("mengambil utk orang lain"), **meninggikan, menjatuhkan** — all standard.
- `imbuhan-di` "tulis → ditulis (NOT ditulisi)": *ditulisi* (menulisi = "menulis pada") is a real word, but the line's point is "di- replaces meN- with no extra suffix" — correct as a rule for forming the passive of *menulis*; left.
