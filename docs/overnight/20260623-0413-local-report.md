(present on the empty landing → one real landing arrow, like the picker guides). Keep
every card's title/body/example verbatim (no new prose → zero confident-wrong risk).

- **Why A over B/C:** B (gate ▶ off on empty) hides the tour from the exact new student

  future attended cycle.
