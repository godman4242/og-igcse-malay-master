## Root cause (instrumented live, not from memory)
`dock()` / `reapplyDock()` (`guideController.js`) compute the correct along-edge `left` and call
`positionBox` — confirmed by a throwaway debug spec logging `computedLeft: 12` (left drop) and
`128` (right drop). BUT the final `getComputedStyle().left` read `70px` with `leftPriority:""`:
**driver.js re-writes the popover's inline `left`/`top` with NORMAL priority to its own CENTRED
placement (70 = the 250px box's `(390-250)/2` midX) on every step render, AFTER our reapply
`requestAnimationFrame`.** Whenever driver won that raf race, the off-centre drop collapsed to the
edge centre — exactly the "environment-sensitive in headless chromium" symptom (the race resolves
differently per environment). A genuine flaky **product** bug.

## Fix (timing-independent · surgical · 2 files, +23 lines)
1. `src/lib/guide/guideController.js` — `positionBox` ALSO publishes the position as CSS custom
   props `--guide-dock-left` / `--guide-dock-top`; `restoreDefault` clears them on full reset.
2. `src/index.css` — `.guide-docked` pins `left`/`top` (+`right:auto`/`bottom:auto`) FROM those
   vars with `!important`. Important-author beats driver's normal-inline `left`, so the docked box
   obeys our computed position regardless of who writes `style.left` last or in what raf order.

The vars are inert while floating/un-docked (the `!important` rule is scoped to `.guide-docked`),
so free-drag, keyboard-dock, resize, and double-click-restore are untouched.

**Decision / veto:** chose CSS-`!important`-via-custom-prop over a controller-side
MutationObserver re-apply (heavier, loop-risk) or a double-raf (still a race) — the cascade
guarantee is deterministic; the alternatives are not.
