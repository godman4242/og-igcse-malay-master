## Web verification (DBP, not memory)

`pengaman` — [prpm.dbp.gov.my](https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?keyword=pengaman):
- Kamus Dewan Edisi Keempat: *"orang (pihak) yg mengamankan"* (one who pacifies/secures).
- Kamus Pelajar Edisi Kedua: *"orang, pihak dsb yg mengamankan: tentera ~"* — canonical example *"tentera pengaman"*
  = peacekeeping/security forces.

So `pengaman` = the peN- **agentive** of `aman` ("one who makes peaceful") = peacekeeper/securer — NOT the occupational
"security guard" who guards premises (that is `pengawal keselamatan` / `pengawal`).

## Fix (surgical, 1 data line)

`meaning: 'security guard'` → `meaning: 'peacekeeper/one who secures'` (word `pengaman`, type `peN-`, pos `noun` all
kept — it is a genuine peN- agentive of `aman`, so fix the gloss, don't remove the word). Matches the family's own
`mengamankan` ("to secure/pacify") / `keamanan` ("peace/security") glosses.
