# IGCSE Malay Master — User Guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through everything the app can do and how to use it to actually get a better grade. You don't need to read it all at once — jump to whatever you're working on.

Works for **IGCSE Malay (0546)** and **IGCSE English (0500 / 0510)** — most screens have a **Bahasa Melayu / English toggle**.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Open the app** at **https://upg-igcse-malay-master.vercel.app** — you can start straight away as a **guest**, no account needed.
2. **Pick your topics** and a **daily goal** (10 / 20 / 30 / 50 cards) the first time.
3. **Sign up** (free, one tap) whenever you want your streak, XP, and progress to **follow you across phone and laptop**. Without an account your progress is saved only in that one browser.

> 💡 **Install it like an app.** On your phone, choose "Add to Home Screen". It then opens full-screen and works **offline** — perfect for revising on the bus.

---

## 2. The Dashboard — start here every day

The home screen is your control centre. The most important part is the **Daily Plan** at the top: instead of making you decide what to revise, it gives you an **ordered, time-budgeted list** — usually:

1. Cards that are **overdue** for review
2. **Fix-ups** (mistakes you keep making)
3. An **exam rehearsal** if one is due
4. One **skill focus** for the day (your weakest of speaking / writing / grammar)
5. Foundation practice

Just start at the top and work down. You'll also see your **streak 🔥**, **readiness %**, recent performance, and your weakest areas.

---

## 3. Study modes (vocabulary)

Tap **Study** to review your flashcard deck. The app uses **FSRS-4.5 spaced repetition** — it shows each card at the perfect moment for memory, so you spend time only on what you're about to forget.

After each card you rate it **Again / Hard / Good / Easy**, and the app schedules the next showing automatically.

**Six ways to study the same cards:**

| Mode | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Flashcard** | Flip the card, recall the meaning, rate yourself. |
| **Quiz** | Pick the right answer from options. |
| **Type** | Type the translation — strongest for spelling. |
| **Listen** | Hear the word, recall it. |
| **Cloze** | Fill the missing word in a sentence. |
| **Speak** | Say the word aloud; get **pronunciation feedback** (green = right, red = off) with tips. |

**Smart Study** mixes vocab, writing, and speaking in one session (this "interleaving" is proven to build stronger long-term memory than blocking one type).

### Where cards come from
- The built-in **dictionary** and **topic packs**.
- The **Word Family explorer** — learn a root and all its related forms together.
- **Import** — paste any Malay text, tap words you don't know, and turn them into cards instantly.

## 4. Speaking practice (Paper 3 oral)

This is one of the app's strongest features. Two ways to practise:

### Roleplay (the exam simulation)
Tap **Roleplay**, choose a scenario (e.g. *Kapal Terbang*, *Telefon Terpakai*). The app plays the **examiner**, gives you a prompt each turn, and you reply by **typing or speaking**. You get feedback every turn and a scorecard at the end. 15 Malay + 7 English scenarios.

### Topic speaking
Tap **Speaking**, pick a topic, and **record yourself** answering. You'll get:
- a **live transcript** as you talk,
- a **band (1–6)** from a calibrated examiner rubric,
- specific **fixes**, an **improved version** of your answer, and **vocabulary upgrades**.

### 📈 Speaking Progress (track your improvement)
On the Dashboard, the **Speaking Progress** card shows whether you're actually getting better:
- a **band trend** (e.g. *best 5 · recent avg 4.2 · ↑ from 3*) with a mini-graph once you have a few attempts,
- your **top recurring weakness** (e.g. "few connectors") with a one-line fix,
- **"due for another go"** — the topics you scored low on or haven't practised in a while. Tap one to jump straight back in.
- an optional **AI coach summary** — if you've added your own AI key (see §12), tap it for a short, personalised "here's your trajectory and the one thing to drill next."

> 🎙️ **Tip:** Speaking practice needs microphone permission and works best in Chrome. If your browser doesn't support it, you'll see a friendly note.

## 5. Grammar drills

Tap **Grammar**. Instead of static tables, you get **interactive drills** that are spaced like flashcards so the rules actually stick:

- **Malay:** *imbuhan* (meN-, ber-, di-, -kan, -an), tense markers (sudah / sedang / akan / belum), and more.
- **English:** confusables, subject–verb agreement, articles, and others.

Get one right and it comes back later; get it wrong and it comes back sooner.

For anything you don't understand, open **Cikgu Maya** (see §9) and just ask.

---

## 6. Writing analysis

Tap **Writing**, choose one of **21 IGCSE formats** (letters, articles, speeches, reports, narratives… for both languages), and write or paste your response. You'll get:
- an instant band estimate,
- detailed, sentence-level feedback,
- a **band-6 exemplar paragraph** for that exact format to model your style on.

---

## 7. Reading comprehension

Tap **Comprehension** to read a passage and answer questions (the app can generate questions in both languages). Great for building the close-reading speed Paper 1/2 demands.

---

## 8. Listening practice (Paper 4)

Tap **Listening**. A passage is **read aloud** to you (with a **replay limit**, just like the real exam) and you answer questions on it. Trains you for exam conditions, not just casual listening.

---

## 9. Cikgu Maya — your AI tutor

Tap **Cikgu** to chat with a built-in grammar and exam tutor. Ask things like *"When do I use ber- vs meN-?"* or *"How do I start a formal letter?"* It answers instantly from a built-in knowledge base, and (when available) can give richer AI explanations.

---

## 10. The Mistake Journal

Every mistake you make — in writing, speaking, grammar, comprehension, anywhere — is quietly **collected in one place** (tap **Mistakes**). The app:
- groups them so you can see your **patterns**,
- escalates the ones you repeat,
- and **automatically turns your important vocab/grammar mistakes into flashcards** so you're forced to actually fix them.

This is the loop that stops you making the same error in the exam.

---

## 11. Exam rehearsal & readiness

Tap **Exam Rehearsal** for a **timed, ~30-minute mock** that blends comprehension, writing, and speaking into a single **Readiness %**. Set your exam date in **Settings** and the dashboard countdown + daily plan will adapt as it approaches — more review early, more exam-shaped practice near the end.

## 12. Use your own AI key (optional)

By default, the app's AI features (Cikgu, writing feedback, speaking grades, comprehension questions, the speaking coach) just work. If you'd like them to run on **your own** account instead — for example to use them as much as you like — you can add a free **OpenRouter** key:

1. Get a free key at **openrouter.ai**.
2. In the app, go to **Settings → "Use your own AI key"** and paste it in.
3. Tap **Test key** — you should see **✓ Working**.

From then on, all AI runs on your key. It's **stored only in your browser and never sent to our servers**, and you can **Clear** it any time. You don't need this to use the app — it's purely optional.

## 13. Tips for the best results

- **Show up daily, even for 10 minutes.** Spaced repetition rewards short, frequent sessions far more than rare cramming.
- **Trust the Daily Plan.** It already knows what's most urgent — just start at the top.
- **Use Speak/Roleplay out loud.** Speaking is where most students lose marks because they only ever practise silently.
- **Don't skip your Fix-Ups.** Those are your real weak points, scored by you.
- **Do a rehearsal weekly** to keep your Readiness % honest.
